## The Setup

A CEO reached out with a straightforward concern:

> "I hear all the time that Agentic AI and Generative AI is the future. Every day I get emails from different teams asking for funding for moonshot projects. Is the juice worth the squeeze?"

Fair question. Let's break it down with data and real examples.

## The Data Tells a Story

If you go to trends.google.com and search for "Agentic AI" or "Generative AI" with worldwide geography over the past 5 years, the interest curve is unmistakable. A value of 100 represents peak popularity — and both terms are hitting that ceiling. This isn't a fad. It's a global shift.

## The CEO's Priorities

When asked about business priorities, the CEO identified four:

1. **Cost Efficiency** — Reduce manual workloads, free human capital for innovation
2. **Speed** — Processes that took days should happen autonomously and continuously
3. **Strategic Agility** — Novel insights and early risk/opportunity detection
4. **Consistent Quality** — Processes followed exactly, every time

Let's tackle the first one: **Cost Efficiency**.

## Customer Care: A Prime Example

Your customer care is world class — but it can be smoother. Here's how Generative AI transforms it:

### The WhatsApp-Style Experience

What if customer care was as smooth as texting someone on WhatsApp? A bot answers in real-time, identifies when it can't help, and smoothly brings in a live agent in less than 30 seconds.

During the handover, the agent gets a high-level understanding of the issue via AI-powered **text summarization**. They answer the query in near real-time. Then a summary with next steps goes to the customer via text and email.

It's as simple as ordering a pizza.

### Automated Rescheduling

Customer couldn't make a meeting? A generative AI-powered agent calls them, reschedules, and blocks calendars for both sides — no human needed.

### Handling Scale: The Big Sale Scenario

Imagine you launched a massive sale. Agents are flooded with calls. AHT (Average Handling Time) is increasing. FTR (First Time Resolution) is dropping. Resolution Rate is decreasing.

What if Generative AI-powered customer care receives calls on your behalf?

- **Decreases AHT** — faster responses, no hold times
- **Increases FTR** — accurate answers from knowledge bases
- **Increases Resolution Rate** — consistent, policy-compliant responses

These metrics reflect directly on CSAT scores. All while serving a large number of customers at a fraction of traditional contact center costs.
